01 / How it works for you

most of the front desk.

Every agent shares the same multimodal core — retrieval over your own intake forms, policies and FAQs, memory, and voice understanding. For a clinic or services practice, two roles do almost all the work.

Reservations Agent

The bookings desk that never closes

Checks real availability before promising anything, then books, reschedules or cancels an appointment only once the patient replies CONFIRM. No overbooked slots, no double-booked providers, no phone tag over a reschedule.
