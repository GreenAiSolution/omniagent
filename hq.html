The model, confirmed

One engine. Every tenant. No middleman for you to build.

Customers never touch infrastructure — not n8n, not OpenAI, not a database. You own all of it, on your billing, and every tenant's data is walled off inside it.

Customer's WhatsApp number→ Your hosted engine (n8n)→ OpenAI+ MongoDB Atlas (isolated per tenant)

What changed: the original design ran on each customer's own n8n/OpenAI/MongoDB — great for a "your data never leaves your hands" pitch, hard to sell to a small-business owner who doesn't want three new accounts. Now it's the opposite: you host it, you pay OpenAI and MongoDB directly, and the pitch becomes "nothing for you to run" instead of "here's three accounts to set up." The trust story shifts from tenancy to isolation — see the site's Trust section, already updated.
