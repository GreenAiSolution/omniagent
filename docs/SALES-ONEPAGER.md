## The problem every business has

Customers message at 11pm, on weekends, mid-rush. Most of those messages are
simple — hours, pricing, "is this in stock?", "can I move my booking?" — but they
arrive faster than a human can answer, in whatever form is convenient: a typed
question, a voice note from the car, a photo of a receipt, a PDF invoice.

Miss them and you lose the sale, the booking, the goodwill. Staff them and you're
paying people to answer the same questions all day and night.

## The product

Omniagent puts a **production-ready AI agent** on your existing WhatsApp Business
number. It:

- **Understands everything** — text, voice notes, images, PDFs and spreadsheets, all
  in one thread.
- **Answers from *your* knowledge** — retrieval over your own menus, policies, price
  lists and FAQs, with inline citations. It won't invent a policy or a price.
- **Remembers the conversation** — customers never have to repeat themselves.
- **Takes real action** — books a table, creates a QuickBooks invoice, opens a support
  ticket, pushes a lead to your CRM.
- **Asks before it commits** — anything irreversible or money-moving waits for a typed
  `CONFIRM` from a human. One approval, one action.

No new app for your customers. No new tool for your team. It rides on the number
they already text.

## Why it's safe to put in front of customers

- **Grounded** — every factual answer is retrieved and cited; unknowns escalate instead of hallucinate.
- **Gated** — write actions require a human `CONFIRM`, enforced in the agent's system prompt.
- **Isolated** — fully hosted, nothing for you to run. Your knowledge base and conversations are walled off to your business alone; nothing trains on them; cancel and it's deleted.
- **Tunable** — turn write tools off for a read-only bot, tighten approvals, or cap costs by switching models. All configuration.
