# The Omniagent System — Internal Offer Spec

*The unpriced tier. This file is internal: what it actually is, what it actually costs
to deliver, and how to price it per application. Never publish numbers from this file.*

## What the member gets
- **All five agents** (Concierge, Support, Bookkeeper, Reservations, Sales Qualifier),
  personas tuned to the business's voice, plus up to 2 custom personas.
- **Full Night Shift** — Auditor, Chaser, Reputation Loop — tuned schedules, tuned tone.
- **Custom tooling** — up to 4 bespoke tool integrations against their real systems
  (POS, PMS, ERP, calendar, payment links), built and maintained.
- **Human ops desk** — a named human reviews every CONFIRM-gated action queue daily,
  and every escalation within business hours. This is the expensive part; it is also
  the reason The System can be trusted with more autonomy than the tiers below.
- **Quarterly tuning** — retrieval audit, prompt refresh, cost optimization, new-feature
  adoption, and a written state-of-the-system report.
- **The card.** Physical, numbered, gold on black. It costs $9 to print and does more
  marketing than the website. Members show it to other owners. That's the point.

## Scoping questionnaire (the "application")
1. What's your monthly inbound message volume, roughly?
2. Which systems run your business (bookings, payments, accounting, CRM)?
3. What do you want handled *without* you — and what must always reach you?
4. How much revenue leaks today from missed messages / silent quotes / no-shows?
5. Who on your team owns this relationship?
6. Why now?

Answers 3, 4, and 6 tell you whether they're a member or a Scale client. The System is
for owners delegating a function, not buying software.

## Pricing (internal — say nothing before scoping)
- **Floor: $1,500/mo + $5,000 build.** Below this, sell Scale instead — the ops desk
  math doesn't work.
- Standard shape: $2,000–$3,500/mo + $5,000–$12,000 build, scoped by tool count and
  message volume. Anchor annually ("members commit to a year — the tuning cadence
  is quarterly, and quarter one is calibration").
- **Capacity cap: 12 members per operator, hard.** The "004/012" on the card is real.
  Scarcity is only worth something if it's true.

## Delivery economics (per member, steady state)
- Ops desk: ~30 min/day of human review ≈ 11 h/mo
- Quarterly tuning: ~8 h/quarter ≈ 2.7 h/mo
- At $60/h loaded: ~$820/mo cost → at $2,500/mo: **~67% margin**, before build fees.
- 12 members ≈ $30k MRR ≈ $360k ARR from The System alone, at one operator + one
  part-time reviewer. This tier is the valuation engine — see VALUATION.md.

## Rules that keep it premium
1. Never quote a price before the questionnaire is answered.
2. Never discount; adjust scope instead.
3. Publicly reject unfit applicants politely ("take Scale") — every "no" is marketing.
4. Ship the physical card on day one of membership, before anything else works.
