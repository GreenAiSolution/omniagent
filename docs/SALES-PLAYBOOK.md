# Omniagent — Sales Playbook

The pitch in one line: **"Your customers already text you. Now something texts back —
and it never makes things up."**

## ICP — who buys this fastest
1. **Restaurants, salons, clinics, tour operators** — booking-heavy, WhatsApp-native
   customers, real money lost after hours. Lead with **Reservations + Reputation Loop**.
2. **E-commerce / retail with WhatsApp support** — ticket volume pain. Lead with
   **Support Agent** ("send me a photo of a broken product right now, watch it handle it").
3. **Trades & agencies that quote** — money dies in silent quotes. Lead with **The Chaser**
   ("how many unanswered quotes are in your phone right now?").

Disqualify fast: no WhatsApp volume, no documents/policies to ground on, or wants the
bot to "just figure out" prices. Grounding is the product; nothing to ground = no sale.

## Outbound (WhatsApp-first, of course)
**Opener (send from your own Omniagent number so the demo IS the channel):**
> "Quick question — who answers your WhatsApp at 11pm? Ours answered this message.
> I put AI staff on business WhatsApp numbers: bookings taken, quotes chased, reviews
> grown. 20-min demo on your own menu/pricelist — worth a look?"

**The Chaser hook (trades/agencies):**
> "Rough math: if you send 20 quotes a month and 30% go silent, that's real money in
> 'meant to reply'. I run an agent that follows up politely on day 3, 7, 14 — and stops
> the second they answer. Want to see last week's recovered-revenue report format?"

## The 20-minute demo (script)
1. **(2 min)** Ask for their 3 hardest customer questions + their price list/menu (they
   sent it in the booking email — you loaded it beforehand).
2. **(8 min)** Live on WhatsApp, from *their* documents: text question → cited answer;
   voice note → answered; out-of-scope question → watch it **refuse and escalate**
   ("this is the feature — it doesn't guess"); booking or invoice → watch it wait for CONFIRM.
3. **(5 min)** Night Shift: show a sample morning brief and a Chaser day-7 message.
   "This part works while nobody's messaging you."
4. **(5 min)** The ledger (site §06) with *their* numbers → land on Growth. If they push
   for "everything, hands-off," introduce **The System** and the application.

## Objections
- **"AI will say something wrong to my customers."** → It only answers from documents
  you approved, with the source attached, and refuses everything else. Show the refusal
  live — this closes more deals than any feature.
- **"WhatsApp bots feel cheap."** → Cheap bots are menus with buttons. This reads voice
  notes and receipts and remembers the thread. Show, don't argue.
- **"I can build this with n8n myself."** → "Genuinely, you can — the engine is n8n.
  You're paying for the tuned prompts, the CONFIRM gate, the smoke tests, and someone
  who fixes it before you notice. Same reason restaurants don't mill their own flour."
- **"Too expensive."** → Never discount. Move down a tier or add the setup fee to
  monthly ($75/mo × 12). Discounting kills the positioning and The System's mystique.

## Anchoring & The System
Always present all four tiers. The unpriced System does two jobs even when nobody buys
it: it makes $399 feel modest, and it starts "what would it take?" conversations.

**When someone asks the price of The System**, the only answer:
> "It's scoped — depends on your systems and volume. The application is 6 questions;
> if the fit isn't obvious to both of us, we'll tell you to take Scale instead."
(Internal floor: see THE-SYSTEM.md. Never say a number before scoping.)

## Pipeline math to hit the first $8.4k MRR
Working WhatsApp outbound to local businesses converts roughly: 100 openers →
~15 replies → ~6 demos → 2 closes. So ~20 Growth clients ≈ **1,000 targeted openers**
over ~4 months at 50/week — one afternoon a week, or one Omniagent instance doing
its own outreach (drink the champagne you sell).

## Referral loop
Month-2 email to every happy client: "Who else do you know drowning in WhatsApp?
One month free per referral that goes live." Local businesses cluster; three barbershop
clients = the whole street eventually.
