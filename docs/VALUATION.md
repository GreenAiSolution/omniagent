# Omniagent — The $300k Design

*The honest math. A business is worth what its cash flows sell for — small
recurring-revenue service businesses trade at roughly **3–4× ARR** (annual recurring
revenue), productized-SaaS-like ones at the high end of that. So the design target is
simple:*

> **$300,000 valuation ÷ 3.5× ≈ $86k ARR ≈ $7,200 MRR.**

Everything in this repo is engineered so one operator can reach and hold that number.

## Three routes to $7.2k MRR (pick one, or blend)

| Route | Mix | MRR | Time cost /wk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Volume** | 18 × Growth ($399) | $7,182 | ~11 h |
| **Blend** *(recommended)* | 8 × Growth + 2 × System ($2,000) | $7,192 | ~9 h |
| **Premium** | 3 × System ($2,500) | $7,500 | ~8 h |

Plus non-recurring: setup fees ($900 × new clients) and System builds ($5k+) — that's
cash flow for the ramp, not part of the multiple.

## Why this specific design supports the multiple
Buyers of small businesses pay for **transferability, margin, and stickiness**. Each is
built in, on purpose:

1. **Transferable by construction.** Client credentials live in *client* infrastructure
   (their n8n, their keys). The business is: this repo + the playbooks + the client
   relationships. A buyer can operate it from the OPERATIONS.md alone. No founder magic.
2. **~70% gross margins** (OPERATIONS.md unit economics) — model costs are the
   client's, your cost is bounded operator time with written cadences.
3. **Sticky by function, not by contract.** The agent holds the client's tuned knowledge
   base, their review momentum (Reputation Loop compounds), and their receivables
   discipline (Chaser). Leaving means firing a working employee, not canceling a tool.
4. **Documented delivery.** Onboarding checklist, smoke suite, SLAs, churn playbook —
   diligence-ready. The difference between "freelancer income" (worth ~1× ARR) and
   "system" (worth 3–4×) is exactly these documents being true.
5. **A premium tier with real scarcity.** The System's 12-seat cap and ops desk push
   average revenue per client up without pushing operator hours linearly.

## The ramp (from the SALES-PLAYBOOK pipeline math)
- ~1,000 targeted WhatsApp openers → ~20 Growth closes. At 50 openers/week: **~4–6
  months to Volume-route MRR**, faster if two System applications land (each System
  member ≈ 5 Growth clients of revenue).
- Milestones: first 5 clients = proof + testimonials → raise setup fee to $1,200;
  at 10 clients = open System applications; at 18–20 = hold, tune churn under 2%/mo,
  and let 12 months of clean books season the multiple.

## What this file is not
A promise. Valuation follows revenue, retention and clean records — the repo gives you
the machine and the manuals; the openers still have to be sent. But the design math is
real: **hold $7.2k MRR with <2% monthly churn for a year, keep the books clean, and a
$300k conversation is a normal one.**
