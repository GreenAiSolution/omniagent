03 / The night shift

Three services that run
while nobody's asking.

The staff answers when customers write in. The night shift doesn't wait to be spoken to — these are scheduled, self-starting automations that work your business on a clock.

02:00 daily The Night Auditor While you sleep it reconciles the day — bookings vs. no-shows, invoices vs. payments, threads that went unanswered, anything the humans dropped. At 07:00 a one-page morning brief lands on your WhatsApp: what happened, what's stuck, what it already fixed. cron · daily
09:00 + escalating The Chaser Every quote, invoice and proposal that went silent gets a polite, personal follow-up — then a firmer one, spaced like a good bookkeeper would. It stops the moment they reply, and reports recovered revenue weekly. Most businesses find thousands sitting in "I meant to reply." cron · memory
T+1 after visit The Reputation Loop The day after every visit, delivery or job it asks one question: how was it? Happy customers get walked to the public review link. Unhappy ones get intercepted into a private ticket — resolved before they become a one-star. Your rating compounds on autopilot. event · gated

All three run on the same engine, same controls — grounded, gated, logged. Outbound messages respect WhatsApp's messaging windows and use approved templates. Nothing sends that you haven't sanctioned.

05 / Trust & security

Why it's allowed
near your customers.

The distance between a demo and something you'd trust with real people is control. It's not a feature tier, it's the default — and none of this is here to replace your team. It's here to protect their time, and hand off the moment a customer needs a human's judgment instead of an agent's.

No. 1 — Grounding

It cites or it shuts up

Every factual answer is retrieved from your own documents and tagged [source: refund-policy]. Not in your knowledge base? It says so and escalates. It does not improvise policy.

No. 2 — The gate

Money waits for CONFIRM

Anything irreversible — an invoice, a payment, a booking — is summarized back first. The agent acts only after a human types CONFIRM. One confirmation, one action. Every time.

No. 3 — Memory

It remembers the thread

"And the second one?" works. Customers never repeat themselves, never start over, never get asked for the order number twice.

No. 4 — Tenancy

Your keys, your data

Runs on your n8n, your OpenAI key, your MongoDB. No middleman in your customer conversations, nothing trains on them. Delete it and it's gone.

No. 5 — The dial

Risk is a setting

Strip the write-tools for a read-only agent. Tighten what needs approval. Swap models to cap cost. All configuration, no code.

No. 6 — The handoff

Humans get the full file

When it escalates, your team receives the summary, the history, and the customer's number. The handoff loses nothing, and the customer feels it.

09 / Questions

Asked, answered.

Do my customers need to install anything?+

No. It lives on the WhatsApp Business number they already message. Nothing to download, nothing to learn, nothing to notice — except the reply time.

Will it make things up?+

It's built specifically not to. Every factual answer is retrieved from your documents and cited. If the answer isn't there, it says so and offers a human. It will not invent a policy, a price, or a promise.

Does it actually do things, or just talk?+

It acts: books the table, creates the QuickBooks invoice, opens the ticket, pushes the lead, chases the silent quote at 9am sharp. Anything irreversible waits behind a typed CONFIRM first.

What exactly is the night shift?+

Three scheduled automations that start themselves: the Night Auditor reconciles each day and sends a morning brief; the Chaser follows up on silent quotes and invoices until they answer; the Reputation Loop asks every customer how it went and routes the unhappy ones to you privately. Growth includes one; Scale and The System include all three.

Who holds my data?+

You do. It runs on your own n8n instance, your OpenAI key, your MongoDB Atlas cluster. We are not in the middle of your customer conversations and nothing trains on them.

How long until it's live?+

Self-serve: a weekend. Done-for-you: we connect the number, load the knowledge and wire the tools within a few business days. The System: a scoping conversation first — that's the point of the application.

Why doesn't The System show a price?+

Because it's scoped to your business, built to your stack, and capacity-limited by the human ops desk behind it. It is not cheap, and it is not for everyone — the application exists so neither of us wastes the other's time.

What's coming next?+

Three things currently in build, in this order: a Voice Agent for the phone number itself, now that WhatsApp supports native voice calling — same grounded, gated core, spoken instead of typed. A gated checkout, so the agent can take a payment the same way it takes a booking — summarized first, moved only after CONFIRM. And a second channel alongside WhatsApp, most likely RCS. None of this is live yet — this page will say so the day it is.
