Why businesses trust it in production

A chatbot guesses. An Omniagent is accountable.

The difference between a demo and something you'd let talk to real customers is control. That's built into every agent by default.

📚

Grounded, not guessing

Answers come from your own documents via retrieval, cited inline like [source: refund-policy]. If the answer isn't in your knowledge, it says so and escalates — it never invents policy or prices.

🔒

Human-gated actions

Anything irreversible — creating an invoice, taking a payment, booking a table — is held behind a CONFIRM protocol. The agent summarizes the exact change and acts only after a person approves. One approval, one action.

🧠

Real memory

It remembers the whole conversation, so customers can say "and what about the second one?" and be understood — no repeating themselves, no starting over.

🏠

Your data, your tenancy

Runs on your own n8n, your OpenAI key and your MongoDB. No third-party middleman quietly training on your customers. Delete it and it's gone.

🎚️

Tune the risk

Turn write-tools off for a strictly read-only bot, tighten what needs approval, cap costs by swapping models — all config, no code.

🧑‍💼

Clean escalation

When a human is needed, it opens a ticket or pings your team with a full summary and the customer's number — so the handoff loses nothing.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Do my customers need to install anything?

No. Omniagent lives on your existing WhatsApp Business number — the one your customers already message. There's nothing for them to download and nothing new for them to learn.

Will it make things up?

It's built specifically not to. Every factual answer is retrieved from your own documents and cited. When the answer isn't in your knowledge base, it says so and offers a human — it won't invent a policy, a price, or a promise.

Can it actually do things, or just chat?

It takes real actions through wired-in tools — booking a table, creating a QuickBooks invoice, opening a support ticket, pushing a lead to your CRM. Anything irreversible or money-moving is held behind a typed CONFIRM approval first.

Who holds my data?

You do. Omniagent runs on your own n8n instance with your own OpenAI key and your own MongoDB Atlas cluster. We don't sit in the middle of your customer conversations, and nothing trains on your data.

How long does setup take?

A single agent can be live in a weekend. With done-for-you setup we connect your number, load your knowledge and wire your tools for you — typically within a few business days.

What does it cost to run?

Beyond the plan, you pay OpenAI and WhatsApp usage directly at their rates — for a typical small business that's often under a dollar a day. You can cap it further by switching to lighter models; it's all configurable.
